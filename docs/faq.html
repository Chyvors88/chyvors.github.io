01 / WHERE DOES THE LIGHT GO?

Bright face or soft halo?

Both can use LEDs. The difference is where the light comes out.

SIGNGlowing letter face

BRIGHT & DIRECT

Front lit

The letter itself lights up.

Choose it for: A name that stands out clearly after dark.

Good fit: Shopfronts, cafés, retail entrances and signs people need to spot from further away.

Letter size, contrast and position still matter. Light alone does not make small lettering readable.

Wall·Letter→You

The lit face points towards the viewer.

SIGNHalo on the wall

SOFT & ATMOSPHERIC

Back lit / halo lit

Light glows on the wall behind the letter.

Choose it for: A softer, more refined glow around the lettering.

Good fit: Reception walls, boutique entrances and brand features viewed at a closer distance.

The wall colour, texture and gap behind the letters affect the halo. We check the surface before recommending it.

Wall←Letter·You

Light travels back towards the wall. The letter face stays solid.

Easy memory trick: front lit = glowing face. Back lit = glowing outline on the wall. These simplified illustrations show where the light goes.

Does “back lit” always mean a halo?

For raised letters in this guide, yes: it means light behind the letters. A lightbox can also be described as backlit because it lights a graphic panel from behind. Send a reference picture so we know which look you mean.

Do I need lighting at all?

If your sign is mainly seen in a well-lit interior or during the day, an unlit sign may suit your brief. Tell us the viewing distance, opening hours and lighting around the sign. We’ll help you compare the options.

02 / WHAT SHOULD THE LETTERS BE MADE OF?

Colourful and light.
Or a metallic statement.

Material and lighting are two separate choices. A metal letter can be unlit, front lit or halo lit depending on its construction.

SIGNColour-finished aluminium

Aluminium

Start here for lighter, colour-finished signs.

  • A useful choice for larger letters and panels where weight matters.
  • Can be painted or finished to suit brand colours.
  • Often worth comparing for a practical, budget-conscious brief; the actual quote depends on the build.

Indoor or outdoor suitability depends on the alloy, finish, mounting and location.

SIGNBrushed stainless steel

Stainless steel

Start here for a distinctive metallic finish.

  • Choose it when brushed or polished metal is part of the brand look.
  • A good option to discuss for reception lettering and premium storefront details.
  • Generally heavier than aluminium for comparable pieces, so the mounting needs to suit the weight.

Stainless steel resists corrosion. Its grade, finish and care need to suit the site.

Easy memory trick: aluminium for lighter, coloured builds; stainless steel for a metallic look. The right grade and finish depend on the location.

Which material is better outdoors in Singapore?

Tell us whether the sign is sheltered, exposed to rain or near the coast. Humidity, salt, surface finish and fixing details all affect material selection. We’ll recommend a suitable grade and finish for the site, rather than choosing by the material name alone.

Can I combine metal letters with LED lighting?

Yes, depending on the design. For example, a front-lit letter can have a metal body with a translucent face, while a halo-lit letter can have a solid metal face with light directed towards the wall. Send us the look you like and we’ll advise on the construction.

03 / BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A smooth start.
A memorable finish.

Which service should I choose?

Choose Stickers & surface graphics. Need a freestanding promotional message? Choose Banners, boards & printed signs. Need a booth or retail display installed? Choose Exhibition setup & dismantling or Shop windows & retail displays. For a small acrylic welcome sign or product display, choose Acrylic signs & small displays.

Do you plan and manage the whole event?

We specialise in backdrop and signage design, printing and production, delivery, installation and teardown within the agreed scope. We do not provide full event planning or management, venue booking, catering, entertainment, guest coordination or event programme management. You or your event organiser handles those arrangements.

Can you work with my theme or brand colours?

Yes. Share your mood board, colour palette or brand guidelines in your project brief. We’ll use them as the starting point for the design.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Your installation date, venue, approximate dimensions, budget range, logo or artwork and inspiration images help us understand the scope. For exhibitions, include the booth size, organiser’s setup schedule and any venue requirements. It’s okay if some details are still being decided.

How far ahead should I book?

Our standard booking window for corporate and large-scale personal projects is 6–7 weeks before installation. Confirm at least 6 weeks ahead; we recommend 7 weeks to give the design and preparation more breathing room. Requests inside this window are reviewed case by case.

Can you help if my project is only 2 weeks away?

Send us the date and brief and we’ll review what is achievable. Requests 2 weeks or less before installation carry an additional rush fee and have limited materials, finishes and customisation options. Acceptance depends on available capacity and a feasible project scope. We’ll explain the options before you commit.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking before design and production begin. The project scope and payment terms will be set out in your quotation.

Do you handle delivery, installation and teardown?

Yes, we can deliver and install the agreed backdrops, signage and displays. For temporary event setups, we arrange dismantling and collection as stated in your quotation. Share the venue’s access arrangements, installation window and required removal time so we can quote the right scope. Removal of existing or permanent signage is assessed and quoted separately.

What should I prepare for an exhibition installation?

Share the booth dimensions, floor plan, graphics or brand guidelines, product-display requirements and the organiser’s access and setup instructions. We’ll plan the display, transport and installation around the agreed scope. Tell us if you need dismantling or collection too, so these can be quoted.

I still don’t know which sign to choose. What should I send?

Send a photo of the wall or shopfront, an approximate size, your logo, opening hours and a picture of a sign you like. Tell us whether it will be indoors or outdoors. You can describe the feeling—bright and bold, soft and refined, or clean and simple—and we’ll help translate it into a suitable sign.
