FROM DESIGN TO INSTALLATION & TEARDOWN

Your design.
Our setup.

A clear process for your backdrop or signage, from the brief and artwork approval to installation and agreed removal.

  1. 01

    Brief & quote

    Share your date, venue, dimensions and inspiration. We agree the scope and prepare your quotation.

  2. 02

    Reserve your slot

    Confirm the quotation and pay the 50% deposit so we can begin design and production.

  3. 03

    Design & approve

    We develop the artwork and layout with you. Review the design before it goes into production.

  4. 04

    Make & pack

    We produce the approved graphics and components, check the details and pack them for transport.

  5. 05

    Transport to site

    We plan delivery around venue access and the agreed setup slot, bringing the prepared components to site.

  6. 06

    Install & dismantle

    We install and check the agreed backdrop or signage. For temporary setups, we return for teardown and collection at the time stated in your quotation.

A LITTLE PLANNING. A LOT MORE POSSIBILITY.

Save the date. Give your ideas room.

6–7 weeks ahead

Our standard booking window for corporate and large-scale personal projects is 6–7 weeks before installation. Confirm at least 6 weeks ahead; 7 weeks gives us more room to plan the details.

50% deposit to confirm

A 50% deposit confirms your booking before design and production begin. Your quotation sets out the project scope and payment terms.

Need it in 2 weeks or less?

Share your deadline. Priority production carries an additional rush fee, with limited materials, finishes and customisation options. Rush requests are accepted only when capacity and the project scope allow.

Requests inside the standard 6-week window are reviewed case by case. Send your date and brief so we can advise what is achievable.
